About us

We build tools that help teams ship software faster, using a multi-agent squad workflow that mirrors how real engineering teams collaborate.

Our squad includes a Lead, Developer, Tester, Reviewer, Security, DevRel, and Docs member — each with clear responsibilities and hand-off points. Every page in this demo site was planned, built, reviewed, and tested by this workflow.

We believe visibility matters: seeing what each teammate is doing, thinking, and deciding builds trust in automated workflows.
